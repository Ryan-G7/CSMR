# CarRoom-2 source

Room geometry, wood floor, rock walls, emissive fixtures and the packed environment come from the user's `D:\BaiduNetdiskDownload\CarRoom-2.blend`. The original file is read only and is never saved over. Source SHA-256 and conversion details are recorded in `carroom.json`; the exported GLB is sufficient to rebuild without that external path.

The AMG uses the existing articulated `AMG.blend` export, fitted to corresponding source hood vertices (maximum permitted error 2 mm). The duplicate source car is excluded. Rock geometry is preserved and partitioned for culling and lightmap UV generation. Texture inputs retain up to 4096 pixels; unsupported color / roughness / normal graphs are evaluated by Cycles on a UV tile. Three dimensional procedural patterns are therefore approximations.

`RoomWorld.hdr` is derived from the source's packed `hamburg_canal_4k.hdr`, including the authored Mapping rotation; it is not a substituted sky. Original HDR: Poly Haven Hamburg Canal, CC0: https://polyhaven.com/a/hamburg_canal and https://polyhaven.com/license . `DiffuseSky.hdr` carries the diffuse environment, with any extracted solar energy represented separately.

`AgX.png` retains its historical filename but is generated with this scene's **Filmic / Medium High Contrast**, exposure 0, rather than AgX. The real-time LUT applies 0 EV of extra exposure. Cycles bakes the original material/world shaded diffuse color (direct + indirect + color) onto Unity’s final UV2 layout, including geometry-dependent AO nodes, at 256 samples in linear EXR. The bake supersamples the 24 texels/m Unity allocation by eight in each dimension, producing 4096² and 2048² atlases. URP does not multiply the source albedo a second time. Rock / wall surfaces use the baked diffuse appearance to avoid specular leakage from the central probe’s exterior aperture; wood and metal keep view-dependent reflections. Fixtures render as HDR unlit surfaces, with their radiance already present in the Cycles bake. `RoomInterior.exr` is a 2048×1024 Cycles panorama from the room center with the duplicate vehicle excluded; it provides convolved HDR reflections and indoor diffuse SH for the articulated car. A fixed closed-car shadow proxy supplies vehicle visibility on upward-facing floor receivers; VR SSAO is disabled. The moving vehicle is excluded from fixed lightmap shadows. `cycles-lighting.json` binds the bake to source / GLB hashes and the final UV2 layout; stale data stops the build. Rebuild with `Tools/Export-VRScene.ps1`, `Tools/Bake-VRLighting.ps1`, then `Tools/Build-Windows.ps1 -Smoke`. Real-time glass does not reproduce Cycles refraction or all multi-lobe BSDF effects. Headset stereo, comfort and frame rate require device testing.
The desktop walkthrough applies the user-approved reference look with a 2.2x pigment gain and RGB room gains (0.65, 0.6175, 0.572), without replacing the source HDR or changing LUT exposure. These gains are scoped to walkthrough mode.
